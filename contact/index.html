Call the dispatch line

Call (818) 337-2642 at any hour. We take calls by phone only, so there is no form to fill out and no wait for a reply. If someone is in danger or water is near electrical equipment, call 911 or the utility first.

What to have ready

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from, if you know, and whether it has stopped.
  • Roughly how much area is wet and which floor.
  • Whether the water looks clean, dirty or smells like sewage.
  • Your insurance company name, if you want to mention it.

Visit or write

Every call gets a human dispatcher, who asks a few short questions and explains the next safe step. That works the same on a quiet weekday and on a storm night.
