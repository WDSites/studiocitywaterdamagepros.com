What we collect

This site has no forms. We receive information only when you call or email us, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem. Our hosting provider and standard web tools may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your call, dispatch a crew and follow up on the job. We may also use it to improve how the site and the phone line work.

Sharing

To dispatch a job we share the details needed with the restoration professionals who will do the work. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@studiocitywaterdamagepros.com. Third-party map embeds and fonts may set their own cookies under their own policies.
