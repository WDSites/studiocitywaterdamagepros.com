Emergency steps

When water is an emergency, start here

An emergency means water is still coming in, is touching electrical equipment, may be contaminated or has been sitting more than a day. In any of these cases, call before you start cleaning.

Close the main water valve first. Then turn off power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. If water is near outlets, a panel or a gas appliance, call 911 or the utility before calling us. Lift valuables and keep people and pets out of dirty water.

Cost and insurance

What affects the price

No two losses cost the same, and a number quoted without seeing the property is a guess. These factors set the total: the water category, the area affected, the materials involved, the drying days and equipment, and the scope of the rebuild. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Insurance, in plain terms

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a standard homeowners policy. Slow leaks and neglect often are not, and flood is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer. We document the loss with photos, readings and a written scope.

Questions

Water damage questions in Studio City

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Studio City?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Crews are sent same day when available, and no exact arrival time is promised. The dispatcher tells you what to do while you wait.

What should I do first when water is spreading in my home?

Stop the source by closing the main water valve or the fixture shutoff. Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. Lift valuables off wet floors, keep people and pets out of dirty water and call the 24/7 line.

How fast does water damage turn into mold?

The U.S. EPA says to dry water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth. That window is why fast drying matters more than a perfect cleanup later.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Often, when the event is sudden and accidental, such as a burst supply line. Slow leaks and neglect often are not covered, and flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep photos and drying records.

Can you handle a large loss or an insurance assessment?

The crew documents the loss with photos, moisture readings and a written scope, which is what adjusters ask to see. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Is water damage restoration the same as waterproofing a basement or foundation?

No. Restoration repairs damage after water got in. Stopping water from entering again is drainage or foundation work for a different trade, and we say so plainly.

Crews serve Studio City, including the Ventura Boulevard corridor, streets near Radford Studio Center and the canyon roads, and nearby Sherman Oaks, North Hollywood, Burbank, Valley Village and Encino. Universal City is also within the dispatch area.
