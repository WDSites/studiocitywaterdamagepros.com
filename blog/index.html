How to use these guides

Each guide answers one question a Studio City homeowner or renter is likely to ask, from what to do in the first hour after a pipe bursts to how mold clearance works before a home sale. The guides explain the general picture and then point out the local questions to ask your city and your insurer.

They are for planning and learning. If water is spreading right now, do not read first. Close the main valve if it is safe to reach, then call the 24/7 line at (818) 337-2642.

The guides are grouped by what they cover: urgent first steps, drying and repair, mold, and insurance. Pick the topic closest to your situation, and use the cards below to jump straight to it.

Every guide is written without health claims and without insurance promises. Coverage depends on your policy, and mold questions beyond cleanup belong with the right professional.
