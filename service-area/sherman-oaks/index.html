When water is spreading in Sherman Oaks

In Sherman Oaks, water emergencies usually start inside the building: a failed supply line, an overflowing washing machine, a leaking water heater or a ceiling that gives way under a stopped-up drain. Call the 24/7 line the moment you see it, because water spreads into walls and floors within minutes.

Sherman Oaks sits along Ventura Boulevard just west of Studio City, so crews reach most addresses by Ventura or Moorpark. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and the dispatcher explains what to do before anyone arrives. No exact arrival time is promised.

If water is still spreading

Close the main valve

If a pipe failed, closing the main water valve stops the supply. If you cannot find it, the dispatcher will walk you through it. In a multi-story building, also tell the neighbors below.

Check power first

Cut power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. Water near outlets, panels or gas appliances means call 911 or the utility before anything else.

Lift valuables

Get electronics, papers, rugs and small furniture off wet floors. Wet drywall bottoms should be left alone until readings are taken.

Keep clear of dirty water

Water from drains, toilets or outside may be contaminated. Keep children and pets out and wait for the crew.

What goes wrong in Sherman Oaks homes

Supply line and appliance leaks

Washer hoses, dishwasher lines and ice maker tubes fail without warning. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and a quick shutoff limits the spread.

Ceiling leaks from above

Condos and two-story homes have upstairs plumbing over finished ceilings. A stain that grows is water still arriving.

Storm runoff

The Los Angeles River forms the northern edge of Sherman Oaks as a concrete-lined channel. It carries stormwater away, while rain on slopes and streets can still find garages and lower rooms.

Past leaks and hidden damp

A closet or wall that stayed damp after an earlier leak is where mold usually starts, which is why drying is checked with a meter.

Sherman Oaks places and what they mean for water

Sherman Oaks was founded in 1927 and named for Moses Sherman. In 1991 the neighborhood boundary was redrawn to include Magnolia Woods and Chandler Estates, about 45 blocks formerly counted as Van Nuys. If you live in either, you may hear your address described both ways, so give the dispatcher your street and the nearest cross street.

The 1994 Northridge earthquake caused damage in Sherman Oaks, and older plumbing can be stressed by a long history of movement and repairs. If your supply lines are original, a visual check under sinks and behind appliances once a year costs nothing and catches slow drips early.

Sherman Oaks Castle Park and the Van Nuys Sherman Oaks Park are useful landmarks when you are giving directions to a crew. Hillside streets reaching up toward the Laurel Canyon side drain differently from the flats, so tell the dispatcher whether the water is coming from above or from inside.
