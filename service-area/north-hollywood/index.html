North Hollywood neighborhoods and water

North Hollywood began in 1887 as the townsite of Toluca, laid out by the Lankershim Ranch Land and Water Company, and it took the name North Hollywood in 1927. Early farms grew fruit and nuts, helped by a high water table near Tujunga Wash. That history is a reminder that moisture can stay near the surface in low spots, so check garages and ground-level rooms after wet weather.

Today the area includes the NoHo Arts District around Lankershim Boulevard, the Valley Plaza district built in the late 1940s and 1950s, and the Metro B Line terminus that opened in 2000. Crews reach most addresses along Lankershim, Magnolia and Burbank Boulevards.

Renters, landlords and water emergencies

In the 2000 census about three quarters of North Hollywood housing was renter-occupied. That changes what to do in a water emergency. Tell your landlord or property manager right away, take dated photos, and keep receipts for anything you pay for. EPA advises renters to report leaks and moisture problems immediately to the building owner or manager.

If you own a unit in a condo or a duplex, the shutoff may be shared. Ask the building where the unit shutoff is before you need it.

When water is spreading in North Hollywood

A leak in a multi-unit building can travel through ceilings, walls and shared pipe chases and hit several units. Call the 24/7 line the moment you see it. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and the dispatcher explains the safe first steps for your building.

Find the unit shutoff

Close the shutoff under the sink or at the unit's valve. If it is shared, call the manager while you call the line.

Stay out of the dark

If lights flicker or water is near outlets, stay out and call 911 or the utility before the restoration line.

Protect belongings

Lift furniture and papers onto dry surfaces. Take photos for your landlord and your insurer.

Warn the neighbors

Water that drops through a ceiling hits the unit below. Knock and let them know.

What North Hollywood properties run into

Multi-unit leaks

Water from an upstairs bathroom or kitchen shows up as a ceiling stain below. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for these.

Older bungalow plumbing

Homes from earlier decades may still have original supply lines and aging drains. Check under sinks and behind the water heater.

Ground-level dampness

Garages and ground-floor rooms near low spots can stay damp after heavy rain.

Commercial space

Shops and restaurants near Lankershim need fast cleanup to reopen safely.
