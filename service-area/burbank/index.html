Older Burbank homes and what water does to them

More than 62 percent of Burbank housing was built before 1970. In Magnolia Park, most homes date from the 1940s, when tracts were built for World War II veterans. Houses of that age often have original or patched plumbing, older drywall or plaster and wood framing that has absorbed decades of small moisture events.

That matters in a water loss. Older materials can tell you more about hidden damage if they are probed and metered rather than assumed sound. A crew checks baseboards, trim and framing before deciding what to dry in place and what to cut out.

A separate city with its own offices

Burbank was incorporated in 1911 and was the first independent city in the San Fernando Valley. It has its own water and power utility, Burbank Water and Power, and its own building permit office. If a repair needs a permit, you go to the City of Burbank, not the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety that handles Studio City.

For a main-line break in the street, call the city utility. For water inside the home, call the 24/7 line.

When water is spreading in Burbank

Call as soon as you see water. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and the dispatcher gives you the first safe steps. Crews travel east along Riverside Drive or Ventura Boulevard and Barham Boulevard, depending on where you are.

Shut off the water

Close the main valve or the fixture shutoff. Older homes can have stiff valves, so do not force one that will not turn. Call the line instead.

Power safety

Turn off power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry. Water near outlets or gas appliances means call 911 or the utility first.

Document and lift

Photograph the damage, then lift belongings. Hardwood and plaster in older homes need to be reviewed with a meter.

Avoid contaminated water

Old sewer lines can back up. Stay out of any dark or smelly water.

Burbank neighborhoods and landmarks

Magnolia Park

Mostly 1940s homes. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and a moisture check is wise after any older-pipe leak.

Rancho and equestrian streets

The Rancho district near Griffith Park allows horses and has larger lots. Water often enters through outbuildings and long exterior runs.

Studio and airport corridors

Walt Disney Studios, completed in 1939, Warner Bros. and Hollywood Burbank Airport anchor a commercial area that needs quick cleanup to reopen.

Foothills near the Verdugo Mountains

The mountains form Burbank's northern edge. Homes at the foothills see runoff behavior different from the flats.

Who handles permits for repairs in Burbank?

The City of Burbank has its own building permit office. Ask them whether your repair needs a permit before work starts.

Is it a Burbank water utility problem or mine?

Water in the street or at the meter belongs to the utility. Water inside the home or on your side of the meter is yours. The dispatcher can help you tell the difference.
