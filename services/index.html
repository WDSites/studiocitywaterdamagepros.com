How the services fit together

A water loss moves through stages, and each stage has its own service. First the water is stopped and removed. Then the structure is dried. Then debris is cleared and surfaces are cleaned. Finally what was removed is rebuilt. Mold remediation sits beside that path for the cases where moisture sat long enough for growth.

Studio City crews handle all stages, so you do not have to coordinate separate companies. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

What drives the price

  • The water category, from clean supply water to sewage.
  • How large an area is wet and how many floors are involved.
  • The materials, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall or plaster.
  • How many days of drying equipment the structure needs.
  • How much rebuilding follows the drying.

A number given without seeing the property is a guess, so the dispatcher explains these factors instead of quoting a figure over the phone.

A note on reading the list

Water damage rarely stays in one category. A burst pipe starts as emergency removal, moves to mitigation and cleanup, and ends with repair, and a damp spot that lingers can turn into a mold job. Use the table as a map and not as a rule, and let the dispatcher place your situation on it.

If you already know a plumber is fixing the source, say so on the call. The restoration plan then focuses on the water and the materials.

Before you pick a service

You do not have to choose correctly on your own. Many calls start with one service in mind and end up needing two. Someone who calls about cleanup after a washing machine leak often needs drying readings first, and someone who calls about a musty closet may find the cause was a slow pipe leak. Describe the symptom and let the dispatcher name the service.
